Four kinds of fire

Event Campfire

One link for the whole wedding, party, or reunion. Everyone drops their photos and messages into one warm pile — no app, no account, no "send me the pictures later."

Kindness Fire

Always burning. Leave something warm for the next stranger who wanders in — a kind word costs nothing and lands forever.

Quiet-Hand Room

Always burning. Ask the question you could never ask out loud. Nobody here knows your name, because nobody asked for it.

The Lobby

Gamers passing through. Drop a clip, share a file, talk strategy, move on. Then the fire burns out — and the talk goes with it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Light a room

    One tap. You get a link that nobody can guess.

  2. 2

    Share the link — or the code

    Whoever has the link is in, or they type the room's 5-digit code on this page. Talk, post, share files up to 15 MB.

  3. 3

    Let it burn out — or burn it yourself

    Every created room lives 8 hours. The one who lit it can burn it early. Two standing fires — Kindness and Quiet-Hand — open with the server piece, and up to ten rooms can be lit at once.
