How it works

  1. 1

    Light a room

    One tap. You get a link that nobody can guess.

  2. 2

    Share the link

    Whoever has it is in. Talk, post, share files up to 15 MB.

  3. 3

    Let it burn out — or burn it yourself

    Every created room lives 24 hours. The one who lit it can burn it early. Two fires always burn, and up to ten rooms can be lit at once.
