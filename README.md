# The Campfire

**A chat room that asks for nothing.** No sign-up. No accounts. Share a link,
gather round, talk and swap files. When the fire dies, it's gone.

## What it is

- **Created rooms** (events, gaming lobbies) live **24 hours**, then burn out.
  The person who lit a room can **burn it early**.
- **Two fires always burn:** the *Kindness Fire* (leave something warm for the
  next stranger) and the *Quiet-Hand Room* (ask anonymously, nobody raises
  their voice).
- **Up to 10 created rooms** can be lit at once on the free version.
- Files up to **15 MB** — images, clips, documents.
- A **feedback page** where visitors can tell the builders what's broken or
  missing.
